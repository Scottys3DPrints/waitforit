Wait For It Clips

Short clips from podcasts and comedy, credited to the people who made them.

Wait For It posts short vertical clips on YouTube and Instagram. Every clip comes from a source that allows reuse (Creative Commons Attribution) or that we have permission to use, and every post links back to the original.

Wait For It Uploader

Wait For It Uploader is a small private tool that uploads our own finished clips to our own YouTube channel on a schedule. It is used only by the channel owner and is not offered to anyone else. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and to read the upload status of those videos. It does not read, collect or store data about other YouTube users.
